DSP LED Spectrum Demonstrator

Play a melody and compare the spectra of sine, square, triangle and sawtooth oscillators. The LED matrix is fixed; only the LED states change.

Try this: choose Sine to see mainly the fundamental. Then choose Square or Triangle to see odd harmonics, or Sawtooth to see both even and odd harmonics. Same average power RMS-normalises the four ideal oscillator shapes so waveform comparisons are not simply caused by different total power. Select the octave of the C-major scale and choose a display range up to 20 kHz.
